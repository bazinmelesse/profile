Drop your images in this folder:

  profile.jpg      your picture (square or close to it, at least 400x400px)
  background.jpg   optional background image (wide, at least 1600px across)

Different file names or types (png, webp) are fine. Just update the `photo` and
`background` paths at the top of script.js to match.

Tip: keep each image under ~500 KB so the page loads fast.
